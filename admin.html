Admin sign in

Enter the admin password to edit the site. This device stays signed in until you sign out.

Notices

Short, important messages shown at the top of the members hub (cancellations, reminders). Keep to one or two.

Events

Past events disappear from the site automatically but stay here until you delete them. Set a start and end time and the event lands at that hour in a parent's calendar; leave both empty and it shows as an all-day one.

News

Newest first on the site. The six most recent are shown.

Sections

Meeting night, time and blurb for each section.

Kit lists

One list per event. Items are typed one per line: start a line with ## for a group heading, put a * in front of essential items, and add a note after a |. Example:
## Waterproofs
* Waterproof jacket | With a hood, not a ski jacket
Gaiters | Recommended

Our team

Name, role and section only. Leave section blank for group roles (Group Leader, Deputy GL, Chair, Treasurer, Secretary). No personal contact details are published.

Before you add photos

The site is public, so anything here can be seen, saved and cached by anyone. Only add photos you have the parents' permission to publish, and avoid naming young people in captions. An uploaded photo is reachable at its own link from the moment it goes up, before you press Save. Removing a photo from this list takes it off the site; the file itself goes when you tidy up below, and neither can un-share a copy someone already has.

Add photos from this device

Pick as many as you like: from a camp, an outing, a meeting. They are shrunk here before they are sent, so a full size photo goes up as a few hundred kilobytes and the original stays on your phone. Give them an album name and they show as one set in the gallery, and open as a slideshow.

Photos

Uploaded ones are already here. You can also paste a link, or the file name if the picture is in the site itself (for example photos/camp.jpg). Give a photo a section to have it show on that section's page as well as in the gallery; leave the section blank for a group photo that shows in the gallery only.

Tidying deletes uploaded files that nothing on the site points at any more. Anything uploaded in the last hour is left alone.

Adventure Skills and badges

The nine skills and the diagram are built in. Edit the wording and the badge positions here.

Donate

Leave the donate link blank to hide the donate card.

Campaigns

Goal and raised are in euro. Update "raised" as money comes in; the progress bar follows.

Other ways to help

Sponsors

Logo is optional: put the image in the zip (e.g. sponsors/hotel.png) and enter the path.

Social media

Shown on the Contact page and at the bottom of More. Add a row per account: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, X, or any other link.

Images

Paste image URLs (e.g. from a photo hosted on the site itself: upload the file into the zip and use its name, like photos/group.jpg).

Venue maps

Each venue gets a small map. "Search text" is what Google Maps is asked to show; "Directions link" is optional (a maps.app.goo.gl share link works).

Reset

Reload the built-in default content into the editor (does not save until you press Save).

Export

Downloads everything currently saved as a JSON file. Send it to Claude to have lists read or edited, or keep it as a backup.

Import

Paste JSON from Claude (or choose a file). Merge keeps what you have and adds or replaces only the parts in the file, matched by id or title. Replace overwrites everything.
