This is a sandbox, not part of the app. The Scouters you add and the boxes you tick are saved on this phone only and are never published. To show someone your rota, use Copy at the bottom and send them the text.

Who is covering what

Pick a section. Meetings for the next eight weeks are listed with that section's events. Tick the adults who will be there. Anything below the number needed shows as a gap, and a camp or an event can need more than a normal night.
